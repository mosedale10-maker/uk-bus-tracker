UK Bus Tracker

Free, live bus tracking across Britain — map, fleet and stop boards in one place.

What is this?

UK Bus Tracker shows real-time positions of buses across the United Kingdom. Data comes from national and regional AVL (Automatic Vehicle Location) feeds, refreshed every few seconds.

How to use it

  1. Search — type a town, postcode or bus registration in the search box.
  2. Map — zoom into any area to see live buses. Tap a bus pin for details, route and timing.
  3. Fleet — browse all Staffordshire operators and tracked vehicles.
  4. Stop board — tap a bus stop on the map for a live departure board (Plus feature).

Free vs Plus

The core tracker is completely free. UK Bus Tracker Plus adds extras for enthusiasts:

  • Live spoken announcements as buses approach
  • Virtual stop departure board
  • 3-day and 7-day vehicle history
  • Add bus photos (after approval)

Plus is £1.99 / month, billed through PayPal, and can be cancelled any time.

Data sources

  • Bus Open Data Service (BODS) — DfT national AVL feed
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles

Contact & feedback

UK Bus Tracker is an independent hobby project focused on Staffordshire and the wider UK network. Feedback, corrections and bus photos are welcome — use the Account section to sign in and get in touch.
